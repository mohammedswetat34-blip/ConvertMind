// 01 — anatomy of a leak

Watch a sale die in four steps.

It happens thousands of times a month on your site — invisibly. Here's the journey every lost customer takes.

01
the arrival

A visitor lands.
You have 5 seconds.

They came from an ad, a search, a referral — money already spent. In the first moments they decide whether your page deserves attention or a back-button.

0:05average time to first judgment
02
the hesitation

They squint at your headline.
Confusion costs.

Vague value props, walls of text, three competing CTAs. Cognitive load is the silent killer — when a brain has to work, a hand reaches for the back button.

57%of visitors leave pages they find hard to parse — industry UX research
03
the distrust

They look for a reason
to believe you — and miss.

No testimonials above the fold. No guarantees near the price. Stock photos where faces should be. Trust isn't a section of your site; it's a feeling your site fails to produce.

81%won't buy from a site they don't trust on sight — consumer trust studies
04
the exit

Gone. And the worst part?
You never knew why.

Analytics shows you that they left — never why. The reasons are psychological, and they're sitting in plain sight on your page. That's the part we read.

~30sis all ConvertMind needs to name every reason
